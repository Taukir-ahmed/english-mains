## Key conclusions

The collection is useful for learning formats, but it cannot support precise official year-by-year weightages without provenance checks. The screenshot tables and PDF reconstructions do not consistently agree. There is no candidate-error dataset here, so the plan describes observed traps rather than claiming error rates or universal mistakes.

The first implementation task is a vetted non-RC bank with complete directions/context and defensible answers. Source item IDs, original question/page references and adaptation status should survive conversion. Entire multi-question sets must stay together in both learning and tests.

## Explanation quality example

The RRB 2025 S39 explanation says unreal situations after as if/as though prefer the past perfect, and changes was unaware to had been unaware. That is not a general rule: present unreality and past unreality require different tense choices, and context matters. Compare the [British Council explanation of unreal time](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/unreal-time). The same file's S40 correctly repairs measures impacts to measures impact but incorrectly attributes that embedded verb to the singular subject extent. This illustrates why explanations need validation as well as answer letters.
