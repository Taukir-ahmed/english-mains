## Review decisions

- Removed alternative common completions such as return/refund and levy/charge from same-word questions rather than treating a plausible second answer as wrong.
- Combination verdicts explicitly record correct/incorrect selection direction and explain every statement.
- The scorer cannot infer a combined answer from just one true statement.
- For swap questions, every offered operation is checked against the complete reviewed model. Wrong operations identify the remaining misplaced words. Semantic review still depends on the authored contexts, not just mechanical agreement with a model.
- Triple/phrase fillers distinguish practical feasibility from commercial sustainability, personal reports from measured evidence, conditional release from release regardless of verification, and association from proof of causation.
- Explanations use positive/negative direction only when supported. Grammar, prerequisites and degree of certainty remain separate clues.
- Nine selected vocabulary terms are added/promoted into the focused reading list, each with a reviewed sentence. Other option words do not automatically become daily reading targets.

Data and browser checks verify structural integrity, combination keys, complete swap repairs, immediate feedback, vocabulary links and saved answers. They do not establish an official difficulty equivalence or guarantee exam marks.
