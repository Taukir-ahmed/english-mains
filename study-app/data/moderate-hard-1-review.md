# First Moderate–Hard extension — 26 October 2026

The first three chapters in the proposed harder sequence are Cloze, Contextual fillers and Errors & correction. Three five-question sets are appended inside each chapter, for 45 new questions and a library total of 765. The same Level selector exposes Moderate–Hard; there is no new tab, timer or UI redesign.

## Construction and review

The local papers supplied multiple-suitable-word cloze, combination fillers and multiple-error grammar formats: SBI Clerk 2023/2024 Q51–57; IBPS Clerk 2023 Q57–60; checklist 53 PDF pp2–5; IBPS Clerk 2024 Q61–67. All new sentences and choices are original. These references establish format relevance, not an official difficulty calibration.

| Sets | Coverage |
|---|---|
| MH-C01–03 | Three complete five-blank passages: limits on evidence, selected populations, conditional decisions, risk-based checks, reserves versus forecasts and completed versus deferred decisions |
| MH-F01–03 | Necessary versus sufficient conditions, numerical proportion versus count, financial dimensions, same-word senses, breadth versus depth, and multiple-fit negation/degree checks |
| MH-G01–03 | Repairs that preserve time/agent/certainty, comparative scope, embedded versus main-clause agreement, inversion, mixed conditionals, participial agency and complete multi-error selections |

Each question stores a reviewed nearest alternative, the reason it is not the answer, and at least two reasoning steps. Close alternatives are evaluated against the full stated scope or intended meaning, not just grammatical possibility. A word that could be true in a different context is not automatically the best expression of the specified conclusion.

Specific review decisions:

- Contingent on, dependent on and conditional on are all accepted where they express the same prerequisite. A false distinction between them is not manufactured.
- Share/proportion and absorb/accommodate are accepted as multiple fits in their stated contexts.
- Negating guarantee and negating preclude can both be correct when an outcome is possible but not required. The negative is applied to each verb separately.
- Tentative evidence can support a possibility while failing to establish it. The alternatives cannot deny support that the paragraph explicitly grants.
- Two separate comparisons must preserve equal completeness and greater reliability; a grammatical alternative that reverses those findings is rejected for changed meaning.
- Past conditions can have present consequences; prepositional to and infinitival to take different complements; passive participial clauses need a different understood role from active ones.
- A simpler repeated address/postal-address exercise was replaced with discount in evidential and bill-purchase senses. Purchasing a bill below face value is distinguished from accepting the bill or changing its face value.
- The obvious repeated confined cue was removed from the cloze introduction. The necessary scope evidence remains in the passage.
- Genuine no-change/no-error answers remain. No change required appears in every repair question, so its presence is not an answer clue.
- Cloze models show only the sentence relevant to the answered blank. The shared passage renderer continues to keep unanswered completions hidden.

## Vocabulary and preservation

Six entries are added/promoted into the focused list: qualify, preclude, suffice, contingent, calibrate and discount. Qualify includes both limiting a statement and meeting requirements because both senses are exercised. Natural examples are shared with Daily reading. The focused total is 76 entries; the full reference list has 275.

Existing question IDs, prompts, options, keys and explanations are preserved. A new hash fixture covers all 720 earlier questions. The older 560-question fixture gained its explicit original ID list, with its stored hash unchanged, so future appends do not accidentally become part of that historical baseline. Existing core tests continue to target their original sets; new sets receive a separate suite.

This label deliberately targets more demanding reasoning than the earlier warm-ups. It is not a claim that all 45 items are equally hard or that they reproduce the exact difficulty of any SBI/IBPS/RRB mains paper. Automated consistency and browser checks cannot establish psychometric difficulty; fresh-paper performance remains the readiness check.
