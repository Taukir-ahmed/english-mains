## Review decisions

- Connector alternatives are complete, grammatically plausible statements; wrong alternatives change a fact, scope, time, modal strength, causal direction or the assigned relationship. An option that preserves only the first link is not enough.
- The permission-continuation item specifies its sole permission condition so provided that and as long as do not silently introduce a sufficient trigger where only necessity was supplied.
- The first rewrite retains the actual jurisdiction-based refusals, rather than replacing that fact with only a generic possibility of refusal.
- Same-meaning paired replacements accept genuine equivalents such as quantified/qualified and measured/limited, or credible/creditable and believable/praiseworthy.
- Word restorations distinguish nearby positive or procedural words by their actual roles. There is a genuine unchanged order; every restoration always offers the unchanged original-label order where applicable.
- Sentence-order distractors generally preserve the opening and much of the clause chain. Where a rearrangement could remain grammatical but change a relative-clause attachment, the intended roles are explicitly stated. These are scope-constrained learning tasks, not claims that every possible alternate English ordering is ungrammatical.
- Stylistic preferences and heavy-phrase movement alone are not grounds for rejecting an alternative. Wrong-order reasons identify broken structure, changed stated roles or an anchor violation.
- Every rearrangement offers No rearrangement required, and one displayed sentence genuinely needs no change. One extra fixed opening plus the five final fixed-anchor items give six anchored tasks.
- The viability sentence asks whether the proposal can be judged viable: it concerns the grounds for a judgement rather than suggesting that actual business viability depends on authors’ optimism.
