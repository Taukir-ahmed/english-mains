## Content review decisions

- Paragraph distractors identify a broken reference or supplied evidence-to-response sequence. The two insertions most vulnerable to valid later elaboration were tightened: the next sentence explicitly refers to the hypothesised systematic difference or the missing decimal mark. The answer does not depend only on preferring explanation before result.
- Matching checks every cross-column combination: nine joins for each two-column or meaning task, eight for each three-column task. Several starts genuinely take more than one ending. Instructions explicitly reject a one-to-one assumption.
- Where meaning depends on a restricted sense, that context is shown before answering: monetary cost and records, only some meaning fewer than all, and the stipulated assertive meaning of denial. A grammatical fragment is not rejected merely because it was not the intended diagonal match.
- Agreement tasks use formal singular neither/each, mass-noun evidence, what-clauses and intervening plural nouns. Sentences may admit both past and perfect completions where no timeline prohibits either.
- Phrasal usage distinguishes separable turn up, put off and turn down from put up with and go over. A full noun object is allowed on either side of a separable particle. Disapproving of brushing off a complaint is not grounds for calling the usage incorrect.
- Genuine idiomatic or lexical synonyms are accepted together. The initial double-edged-sword distractor was removed because an event can have both harmful and helpful effects; the replacement instead conflicts with the explicitly unforeseen outcome.
- The antonym of proscribed is requested as permission without compulsion, so prescribed cannot be dismissed merely for being a less preferred opposite. The question states the intended dimension before answering.
- Disinterested is tied explicitly to an engaged reviewer with no stake in the outcome. Illusory does not establish deliberate fabrication; inconclusive does not establish irrelevance; qualified support does not establish rejection.
- Confusable tasks distinguish effect as a verb from affect, principal from principle, discreet from discrete, and rule-following compliance from obliging complaisance. Insurance/assurance is restricted to fire cover versus a promise; no universal claim about life-assurance terminology is taught.
- Acknowledgement is accepted; the actual error in its candidate is liason. No British/American variant is rejected. Fundamentals such as necessary and accommodation are still maintenance learning; the Moderate–Hard label does not imply each isolated spelling is hard.
- Error counts have zero through four independent faulty phrases. An inverted conditional is not wrongly repaired merely because it omits if. Purpose infinitives after a noun object are distinguished from a prohibited verb complement after defer.
- Hybrid fragment boundaries were tightened so plausible fronting of an intact until/because clause is not incorrectly treated as ungrammatical. Incorrect orders now break an explicit determiner, participle or subject/predicate boundary.
